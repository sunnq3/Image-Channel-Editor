Pattern Scan

Finds repeating patterns in an image at every stripe width and direction, draws them on the picture, and says what each one most likely is: JPEG blocks, a resize trace, a faint overlay, or real texture. Give it several images and it stacks them to bring out a pattern they all carry.

One image to analyse it. Several of the same size to stack them and find what they share. Nothing is uploaded.
Larger images are read at full resolution from their centre, never shrunk.

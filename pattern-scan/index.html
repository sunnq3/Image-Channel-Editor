Pattern Scan

Looks for watermark-like patterns: faint regular ripples laid over a whole image. One image gets scanned at every stripe width and direction. Several images get stacked, which fades the pictures and leaves the pattern they all carry. Compression blocks and resize traces are recognised and set aside.

One image to analyse it. Several of the same size to stack them and find what they share. Nothing is uploaded.
Larger images are read at full resolution from their centre, never shrunk.
